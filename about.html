Who We Are

The Webmasters identity

The official esports organization of the University of Cebu – Banilad, formed under the Webmasters student body.

We compete across Valorant and Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, representing our university with discipline, teamwork and competitive spirit. We are builders, competitors and community — forged under one crest and one banner.
